ABOUT US

Welcome to Keki-Keki Bakery!

Keki-Keki Bakery is a bakery dedicated to making fresh, delicious and affordable baked products. We believe that every occasion deserves something sweet.

We specialize in birthday cakes, fresh bread, cupcakes and cookies. Our products are prepared using quality ingredients and a lot of care.

Whether you are celebrating a birthday, spending time with family or simply craving a sweet treat, Keki-Keki Bakery is here to make your day special.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, tasty and affordable baked products while ensuring customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a trusted bakery known for quality, creativity and excellent customer service.
